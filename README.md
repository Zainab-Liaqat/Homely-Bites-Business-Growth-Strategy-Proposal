
Homely Bites: Business Growth Strategy Proposal

A 6-page business strategy proposal for Homely Bites, a homemade-food cloud kitchen planned for Okara, Pakistan.

This project was prepared as an internship task. It shows how a small food startup can set clear goals, use the 4Ps of marketing, pick the right digital channels, and grow step by step.

About the business

Homely Bites is a delivery-only kitchen (a cloud kitchen) that sells fresh, homemade-style desi food. It is made for busy families, office workers, and working women who love ghar ka khana but have little time to cook.

Menu: chicken biryani, white karahi, daal chawal, and a family meal box.

Key points

Objectives (first 3 months)

Orders: 150 in month 1, 300 in month 2, 500 in month 3
Customer satisfaction: 90% of orders delivered within 40 minutes, calls answered within 30 seconds, rating of 4.5 out of 5 or higher
Repeat customers: 40% of customers order again within 3 months

Marketing mix (4Ps)

Product: a small desi menu with a family meal box
Price: medium and affordable (Rs 180 to Rs 1,100), delivery Rs 60
Place: orders by WhatsApp and phone, own riders, a few nearby areas first
Promotion: opening offer, posters and pamphlets, food photos and videos, word of mouth, and customer reviews

Digital channels: WhatsApp, Facebook, and Instagram

Growth plan

Short-term (0 to 6 months): opening offer, local awareness, reviews, and a loyalty offer
Long-term (1 to 3 years): bigger menu, more areas and a second kitchen in a new city, delivery apps, and a strong brand
